1. dodanie DRF i Djoser w config/settings.py w INSTALLED_APS:

INSTALLED_APPS = [
    "django.contrib.admin",
    "django.contrib.auth",
    "django.contrib.contenttypes",
    "django.contrib.sessions",
    "django.contrib.messages",
    "django.contrib.staticfiles",

    "rest_framework",
    "djoser",
    "api",
]

2. dodanie na końcu settings.py ustawienia mówiącemu DRF, że do uwierzytelniania zostanie użyty JWT:

REST_FRAMEWORK = {
    "DEFAULT_AUTHENTICATION_CLASSES": (
        "rest_framework_simplejwt.authentication.JWTAuthentication",
    ),
}
